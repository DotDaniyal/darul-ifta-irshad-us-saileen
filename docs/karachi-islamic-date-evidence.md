# Karachi Islamic-Date Correction Evidence

On 24 August 2026, the live site displayed **11 Rabi al-Awwal 1448** from the structured source. The Pakistan-specific evidence reviewed for this correction instead confirms **10 Rabi al-Awwal 1448** for that Karachi date. Pakistan’s Central Ruet-e-Hilal Committee decision placed 1 Rabi al-Awwal 1448 on 15 August 2026, which makes 24 August the tenth day of the month.[1]

The implementation preserves the existing explicit `HIJRI_DATE_OVERRIDE` precedence and Karachi Maghrib rollover. It adds a narrowly scoped official Pakistan month decision covering 15 August through 13 September 2026; the correction is not applied outside that confirmed 30-day Rabi al-Awwal interval.
