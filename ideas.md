# Design Exploration — Darul Ifta Irshad-us-Saileen Karachi

## Three stylistic approaches

### Blue Minbar Heritage
**Very Brief Intro:** A dignified contemporary Islamic institution expressed through deep cobalt architecture, parchment surfaces, and finely drawn geometric linework. It feels calm, authoritative, and legible.

**Probability:** 0.047

### Contemporary Madrasa Editorial
**Very Brief Intro:** An editorial presentation with wide margins, scholarly typography, and a restrained blue identifier. It feels like an academic journal translated to the web.

**Probability:** 0.063

### Quiet Courtyard
**Very Brief Intro:** A light contemplative system using sandstone, soft green, and sunlit courtyard imagery. It suggests hospitality and reflection.

## Selected approach — Blue Minbar Heritage

### Design Movement
**Neo-Islamic institutional design**: contemporary information design informed by Islamic architectural proportion, manuscript framing, and scholarly-library calm.

### Core Principles
1. **Authority through clarity:** guidance, rulings, and service paths are direct and visibly structured.
2. **Heritage without imitation:** abstract arches, geometric motifs, and paper texture replace decorative clichés.
3. **Bilingual parity:** Urdu and English receive equally intentional typographic space with true RTL/LTR behavior.
4. **Quiet premium craft:** layered surfaces, restrained gold accents, and low-motion interaction establish confidence.

### Color Philosophy
Official **Irshad Blue** anchors the institution’s voice of knowledge and dependable guidance. Warm ivory provides a paper-like reading ground, muted antique gold is reserved for meaningful emphasis, and ink navy maintains high contrast.

### Layout Paradigm
The home page follows a **processional reading path** instead of a uniform centered stack: a broad identity band opens into an asymmetrical hero, then alternates tall editorial panels and framed service modules. Arched content windows operate as visual thresholds.

### Signature Elements
- A repeating **mihrab-outline frame** around date tiles, cards, and labels.
- **Fine geometric grid lines** and muted rosette points in low-contrast backgrounds.
- A **blue rule with gold seal** divider to mark changes of topic.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions make guidance easier to find: menus move with restraint, filtering responds immediately, copy controls confirm clearly, and forms give calm feedback.

### Animation
Use 180–280ms ease-out interface changes. Sections reveal with a subtle 8px upward fade; hero ornaments may shift by no more than 6px. Nonessential motion is disabled when reduced motion is requested.

### Typography System
**Noto Nastaliq Urdu** serves Urdu headlines and body text with generous leading. **Noto Naskh Arabic** handles Arabic. **DM Serif Display** establishes English editorial headings, while **Manrope** supports English body and interface text.

### Brand Essence
**Darul Ifta Irshad-us-Saileen Karachi offers considered, accessible Islamic guidance grounded in the Qur’an and Sunnah for people seeking trusted scholarly direction.**

**Personality:** Scholarly, reassuring, exacting.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are calm and direct. CTAs name a meaningful next action rather than offering generic prompts.

> “شرعی رہنمائی، اعتماد کے ساتھ”

> “Submit a question for qualified scholarly review.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The supplied official logo is the master mark and remains unchanged. It is paired with a bilingual wordmark lockup, while a simplified arch-and-book silhouette is reserved for the favicon.

### Signature Brand Color
**Irshad Blue — #075A9B**.

## Style Decisions

- Primary content modules use a recognisable mihrab/manuscript outline rather than generic rectangular card treatment.
- The archive, detail, and request flows retain the same ivory parchment, Irshad Blue, antique-gold divider, and geometric-linework material language as the home page.
- Fatwa entries read as catalogued scholarly records, with bilingual titles first and deliberately framed metadata.
- Interior-page heroes carry layered architectural depth through low-contrast mihrab silhouettes, rosette points, and geometric manuscript patterning.
- Antique gold functions as a ceremonial marker for primary actions, scholarly metadata, and topic transitions, never as an indiscriminate accent.
